Common Issues & Solutions

Diagnose your problem with our expert troubleshooting guides.

Phone won't turn on

If your device is completely unresponsive, try these steps:

  1. Connect to a charger for at least 30 minutes
  2. Attempt a force restart (device-specific key combination)
  3. Inspect charging port for debris or damage
  4. Try a different charger and cable
Likely solution: Battery replacement or charging port repair

Touch screen not responding

When parts or all of your touch screen stops working:

  1. Clean screen with microfiber cloth
  2. Remove any screen protector
  3. Restart device
  4. Check for software updates
Likely solution: Digitizer or full display replacement

Rapid battery drain

If your battery life has significantly decreased:

  1. Check battery usage in settings
  2. Close background apps
  3. Reduce screen brightness
  4. Disable unnecessary features (GPS, Bluetooth)
Likely solution: Battery replacement if >2 years old
